How it works

Every day there's one puzzle: five tiles, one ordering rule, three tries. Drag them into order and lock in. Green means correct and locked, yellow means close, grey means try again. Takes under two minutes, usually less than one.

When you're done, share your grid to the group chat. It's plain text, so it pastes cleanly into iMessage, WhatsApp, or Slack — same as the puzzles you already share.

Friends, not followers

Kith only uses contacts to find friends who have the app.

  • Numbers are hashed on your phone before they ever leave it.
  • Names never leave your phone.
  • We never message anyone on your behalf.

You only ever appear on someone's board if you have each other's numbers saved. No public profiles, no follow requests, no strangers.

Circles and join codes

No contacts, no problem. Create a circle, share the code or link, and anyone can join — a family, a group chat, an office. Circles get their own leaderboard, same as friends: today, this week, all-time.
