Privacy policy

Effective 2026-09-11

What we collect

Your phone number (to sign you in), a display name, your timezone, your puzzle results, and, if you allow it, a hashed version of your contacts so we can find friends who already play. We also store reactions and taunts you post to friends. We don't collect location, purchases, browsing, health, financial, or diagnostic data.

How matching works

Your phone hashes each contact's number on your device before anything is sent to us — we never see the actual numbers. We hash our copy again with a secret only our server knows, and compare the results. If you and a contact have each other's numbers saved this way, you both show up on each other's boards. Names never leave your phone; only the hashed numbers do.

What we never store

We never store your contacts' names, their raw phone numbers, or the plain hash of a number. The only thing we keep is a further-scrambled version of the hash, and only so we can tell you're mutually connected with someone. It can't be turned back into a phone number on its own.

How to turn it off

Turn off "Let contacts find me" in Settings and you disappear from everyone's matches immediately; your stored contact hashes are deleted. You can also revoke Contacts access entirely in iOS Settings — the next time you open Kith, we detect that and delete your contact hashes the same way.

How to delete

Delete your account any time from Settings, no email required. It happens immediately and in-app: your profile, results, reactions, taunts, contact hashes, and circle memberships are all removed. Backups that include your data age out and are gone within 30 days.

Cookies

This website (not the app) sets two first-party cookies, kith_ref and kith_circle, when you follow a share or invite link. Each stores only the invite or circle code from that link, lasts 30 days, and is used solely to credit the invite once you install the app — never for tracking, analytics, or advertising, and never shared with anyone. Because they're first-party and carry no personal data, no consent banner is shown for them.

No ads, no data sales

Kith has no ad SDK, shows no ads, and does not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. Your data is used only to run the product: matching, boards, streaks, and notifications.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email privacy@kith.app.
